4. How these sizes were placed — please read before quoting

These are pre-plate gauges and no standard tabulates them. Neither ISO 1502 nor ASME B1.2 covers gauging a thread that will later be coated, and ISO 965-1 clause 12 defines the pre-plate band but not gauges for it. The placement rule below is taken from a standard; applying it to the pre-plate band is an engineering decision, taken deliberately and recorded here so it is never mistaken for a tabulated value.

Placement: Taylor / ASME B1.2 Table 4 convention. The gauge basic size sits on the workpiece limit, with the gauge tolerance applied unilaterally inward. The result:

CheckValue
Legal pre-plate band116.0 µm
GO can accept past the maximum-material limitnone
NOT GO can accept past the least-material limitnone
Widest window the pair passes116.0 µm — exactly the legal band
Narrowest window the shop must hold100.0 µm
Why not the ISO 1502 placement. ISO 1502 offsets the GO zone and places the NOT GO zone wholly outside the band, so the pair can accept threads past both limits. That is by design — it favours not falsely rejecting good parts and spends tolerance on gauge life. On this part it would pass a 135 µm window against a 116 µm legal band, letting a thread through that finishes 18 µm under the minimum pitch diameter after plating. On a coated thread, where 24 µm of the class has already gone to coating spread, that trade is not acceptable. The 8 µm gauge tolerance is the whole cost of removing it.

5. Notes for the gauge maker

  • The 8 µm pitch-diameter tolerance is tighter than the 18 µm ISO 1502 would allow for a ring in this size. If it cannot be held on a Ø70 ring, please say so before manufacture rather than opening it up — the tolerance is doing the work here.
  • Mark the GO wear limit 68.6490 mm on the gauge or its certificate.
  • GO ring is full form; NOT GO ring is truncated.
  • Sizes are quoted to 4 decimal places deliberately. GO nominals are rounded up and NOT GO nominals down; re-rounding to 3 places would reopen an acceptance gap of up to 0.5 µm.
  • These gauges are for the pre-plate condition only. Finished-size rings, if ever required, are larger and are a separate order.
